4. Inspect before you sign

Check every box before signing the delivery receipt. Count the pieces and look for crushed, torn or punctured boxes, bent panels, or oil stains. If anything is wrong, write it on the delivery receipt (for example "damaged crate, bent panel") before you sign, and take photos.

  • Report missing items within 24 hours and damage within 3 business days of delivery, with photos.
  • If damage is noted on the delivery receipt and reported on time, we'll file the freight claim and send a replacement or repair part. Hidden damage found later is handled case by case with the carrier. Damage not noted or reported on time may not be covered.
  • Don't install damaged equipment; installing it can void the warranty and the freight claim.
